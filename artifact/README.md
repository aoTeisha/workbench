# artifact/

`workbench.html` is the whole Workbench page as one file: inline CSS, one inline `<script type="module">`, no build.
It has no doctype/html/head/body tags because the Artifact tool wraps it in a skeleton when it publishes.
It is published with the Artifact tool with `capabilities` from `capabilities.json` (every connector tool the page calls), not served from this repo. Pass `capabilities` explicitly on every publish: omitting it keeps the stored list, which would lack new tools. Each person publishes their own copy (`/setup`).
The `host:` entry is the helper on this PC (`host/server.mjs`). The desktop app carries those calls only for a board open inside the app, and only when the helper is in the app's own config; in a browser tab they fail with `server_not_connected`.
Cards, projects and `machine/*` live on the PC (`data/`, `host/db.mjs`): `pollLocal` asks the helper's `db_read` every 3 s (`{ since: rev }`, a full set only when the revision moved) and `applyLocal` feeds them to `normProject`/`normCard` as before; `writeDoc` sends those paths to `db_write`/`db_delete`, run at once inside the click when nothing is pending for that path, so a move reaches the helper before the `card_start` after it. The page writes them only on clicks (a host write may need fresh user activation): the helper itself writes a card's `session`, and moves back a card whose start failed (`card_start` `revert`). Without the helper (a browser tab) the board shows "Your cards live on your PC…"; an old helper (unknown tool) asks for an app restart. Everything else the page keeps in the artifact db: feeds, triage, handled, feedback, settings. `seed.json` is sample content.
Opened outside claude.ai (`file://`) it shows only "This page needs to be opened on claude.ai to reach your board."

Part 1: data layer, Today, Decide (questions, reviews, Done card), Projects, bar, toasts, keyboard, Context.
Part 2 (this version):
- Board: `lanes | widgets` grid, tiles are buttons that open the card overlay (blocked, review, and the plain card with Priority and Move to).
- Bar: on the left, your day (a strip of today's meetings 08:00-19:00 with a red now mark, then the next meeting and how long until it starts, or "Now: <title> until HH:MM"); click it for the calendar pane: Day (that day hour by hour, now line, free gaps of 30 min or more, overlapping meetings side by side, a meeting opens in Outlook) and Week (the five work days from `config/board` `weekStart`, Sunday or Monday, on the same hours; on the two days off it shows the coming week; today outlined, arrows to other weeks, a day opens it in Day). On the right, usage from the helper's `usage` tool: one meter per window (Week, plus 5 h on plans that have one), the night limit as an orange tick; click it for the usage pane (each window with its reset time, the night limit 50% | 70% | 90% | No limit written to `machine/night`, the night check's last message, what last night used, and a warning when the PC is on battery: a sleeping PC on battery stops the night shift; from 18:00 to 07:00 the meter shows it in the warning colour). One pane open at a time; Esc, an outside click or opening a card closes it. Before the helper has the `usage` tool (the desktop app was not restarted) the pane says so.
- Cards in Decide and opened on the board render at `zoom: .95`.
- Opened card (backlog, ready, doing, done): a Runs on row, Model and Effort drop-downs writing `model` and `effort` (`""` = default, labelled with the defaults `card_sessions` reports). Moving to Doing passes them to `card_start`. While the session works, the chip says what it runs on ("Doing · on Fable high"), and when the card asks for something else, Switch now calls `card_start` with `restart: true`. The New card form has the same row; Claude's draft (Plan) leaves both on Default, so new cards run on the Settings default.
- Dependencies, read from the goal's "after #NNN" / "after #<title>" as the night check reads them (only a card that is not done holds one up): a Ready or Backlog tile that waits gets a "Waits for <title>" line; hovering or focusing a tile rings the cards it waits for and the cards waiting for it; the opened card has Waits for and Holds up rows (each opens that card) and drops goal lines that are only "after #NNN"; a Review card's Looks good line adds "frees <title>" (or "frees N waiting cards").
- Every opened card (overlay) has Delete beside the close button: it deletes `cards/<key>` with a 10 s Undo toast that writes the raw doc back. Disabled in Doing (leaving Doing pauses the session first).
- `handover.output` (the one file a task's cards build; the Plan prompt names it with an `Output:` line in each goal) shows under the headline on Review and Done cards with Open, which calls the helper's `open_file` (documents inside a project folder only; .md in Notepad). With a file, a Done card folds its notes into Details.
- Done cards have no footer (nothing left to set); Reopen in the head moves one to Backlog.
- A Done card with a `handover` or `result` opens read-only (`finishedCard`): headline, shape, `picks` in words (a pick that differs from the worker's says the work still follows the worker's), and `result` open, since it is often the deliverable. Asks, review notes and log are folded. `renderMarkdown` renders `|` tables and `>` quotes.
- Skip: the first moves the item to the back of the deck, the second hides it until tomorrow (`settings.deferred`). Show skipped (Done card) and Show them (Today, when only skipped items remain) clear `deferred` and rebuild the deck.
- Widget column: Mail, Slack, Jira with three views each (the Calendar widget moved to the bar; a saved `calendar` entry is ignored), saved in `settings.widgets`. Reorder by the grip: the lifted widget moves by `transform`, siblings glide (FLIP), the order is saved once on release.
  `+ Add` greys out added kinds ("Added ✓") and is disabled when all three are on the board. The column is not rendered, and the board is lanes only, when `settings.widgetsOn` is false or the list is empty; removing the last widget sets it false (toast with Undo), and the dev menu "Widgets" switch turns it back on (restoring the three defaults if the list is empty) or off (list kept).
- Cards (Decide deck, board overlay, Done) sit in a fixed height band (`--card-min` to `--card-max`, `--ov-min` to `--ov-max`); the body scrolls and the actions stay pinned. Each card keeps the height of its first paint, so Context, Send back, a review card's calls and folds, and Write your own never resize it.
- Send back on a review card opens a note block at the end of the body (the primary button becomes "Send back", disabled until the note has text or a pick changed); Context, Skip and the side Context sheet stay. Esc or pressing Send back again closes it and keeps the draft.
- Review cards (deck and overlay): plan strip (`projects/<slug>.plan.phases` and the cards of the same `phase`), "You asked" (`why`, else the goal's first sentence), then the worker's `handover`: headline, shape, You decide (the worker's `calls`; one opens at a time; the pick can be changed, or the user types their own answer in the box under the options, which becomes the pick from the first letter and is saved as `<topic>: own · <text>`; their text is kept in localStorage until the card is saved; a call whose pick matches no option must be chosen or answered; Looks good writes the picks into `picks`, and the line above the buttons says when one differs from the worker's), The worker decided (`chose`), Not checked, Extra work found (`later`; ticked items become backlog cards on Looks good; Undo deletes them), then folds for the asks (`acceptance` with `how`, `evidence`, `catch`) and Details (`result`, branch, log). The line above the buttons says what Looks good will do. Cards without `handover` show `result` in full. A missing `unchecked` shows as "did not say", never as "checked every part". Opening a call or a fold closes the card's other call and fold; opening Send back closes them too, but the note stays open while a call or fold opens, so a changed pick can go into it. Send back works with an empty note when a pick changed: the new ask is then "Follow my picks: …". Keys on a review card: E types an own answer, Enter in that box closes the call, ↑ ↓ change the open call's pick, Esc leaves the box, then closes the call. Shapes: `CLAUDE.md`, the work skill's "Handover".
- Desk cards in the Decide deck: Your day, one per Needs-you mail, one per Slack conversation; Done writes `handled/<id>`.
- Slack cards: one per conversation (a DM, or one thread or top-level message in a channel) that has a hit (a DM to you or a mention) newer than your own last message in it. Up to 3 earlier messages, yours included, show dimmed; the waiting ones show in full, rendered from Slack's markup (names, `@you + N others`, link labels with the site, emoji, bold, code, quotes; an empty message is a file). The state line says since when it waits, or how many others were tagged; a DM adds the other person's local time from `slack_read_user_profile` (only the `Timezone` line is read, nothing is stored). "In short" is triage's `gist` when it is at least 60% sure. Its id is `slack:<conversation>@<newest waiting message ts>`, so Done (`handled/<id>`), Later and triage apply up to that message and a newer message brings the conversation back as a new item (an open deck follows it to the new id); the Done card lists conversations hidden because you already replied.
- Reply from a desk card: `R` or the Reply button opens a note block at the end of a mail or Slack card; the primary button becomes `Draft reply` (Claude writes it from a one-line note via `sample(prompt, { modelTier: 'default', cache: false, signal })`; without `sample` it opens straight on the text), then `Send` (Slack, `slack_send_message`, threaded for channel mentions; the item is marked handled and the deck advances) or `Save draft` (mail, `outlook_create_reply_draft` / `_reply_all_draft`, html body of `p` and `br`; the card stays and its Open button becomes `Open draft`). Mail is never sent from the page, nothing is retried, errors show inside the block and the text stays. Esc closes it and keeps the text for the same card.
- Triage: Claude on this page (capability `sample`, `sampler.json(prompt, { modelTier: 'quick', cache: false, signal })`) scores the deck and writes `triage/<docId>`.
  Items are `desk().ids` plus blocked/review cards of non-archived projects; each has a state string (meta line, body clipped to 600 chars) and is pending when `hashOf(state)` differs from `triage/<docId>.hash`. One call scores every pending item (40 per call), with the last 7 days of `feedback/<day>` as calibration lines. Answers with an unknown id or a missing or invalid value are dropped; confidence is clamped to 0..1.
  Runs automatically (5 s after the last feed delivery or once `sample` is ready) when items are pending and no triage doc is newer than 30 min, and from the dev menu button **Triage now**; never two at once, and a failed run waits 30 min before the next automatic try.
  Deck order is urgency first (now, today, later); the meta line shows `· now`, Context shows `Claude: reply now (82%)`, widgets sort by urgency and show an `N now` badge. Items without a triage doc count as `today`.
- Drag cards between lanes (mouse or pen, pointer events, 6 px slop so a click still opens the card): Backlog, Ready, Doing and Done cards drag; valid lanes outline (`drop-ok`), the lane under the pointer lights (`drop-on`); Ready and Doing need an open acceptance item; Blocked and Review are worker lanes and neither drag nor accept. The drop calls `moveCard` inside the `pointerup`, so a drop on Doing also hands the card to Claude. Esc cancels. Touch uses the card's Move to.
- Card sessions: moving a card to Doing (Move to, or a drag; needs an open acceptance item) calls `card_start` on `host:workbench-host` inside the gesture: the PC helper starts the card's own Claude background session in the project folder, or resumes the one in the card's `session` field. Moving it out of Doing calls `card_pause` (`claude stop`, conversation kept). The card moves at once and moves back with the reason when the call fails (browser tab, app cancelled the call, folder not trusted). A project without a folder does not move to Doing. In a folder that is not a git repo a second card waits in Doing (the helper starts it when the first hands the folder on). A card with a session shows **Open session** (`card_open`: the live session in a Windows Terminal window), and Doing tiles show one word from `card_sessions` (working, needs you, waiting, idle, stopped). When a Doing card's session needs you or went idle, `card_sessions` adds `why` (the session's last words, its TLDR line, read from the tail of its transcript), shown at the top of the opened card ("Why its session needs you", warm; "Where its session stopped") and as the tile's hover text, so a session that could not write to the board still says why.
- Dev menu: the cog at the right of the bar (480 px wide) opens Cards run on (Model and Effort for every card that leaves its own on Default, by day and by the night check; their `Default (…)` is Claude Code's own default; fixed select widths so the row never changes height), Ultracode (a switch: card sessions start with ultracode on, planning multi-agent workflows; both live in the shared `machine/cards`, and a card's Runs on labels its Default with them), Links open in (App | Browser, `settings.linkTarget`, default browser; Test opens `slack://open` through the same anchor so you can see whether the sandbox passes custom schemes), Triage (note `Claude on this page · last run <when> · <n> items` and the **Triage now** button; `Scoring <n>…` while running, `Rate limited, try later`, `Couldn't score · <code>`; `not_granted`, `sampling_disabled`, `not_declared` hide the button and say `Claude isn't available on this page`; the note is one line with an ellipsis so the menu never changes height), Keep awake (a switch, stored in `settings.keepAwake = { on }`; the PC will not sleep, the screen still locks; a change calls `keep_awake_start` / `keep_awake_stop` on `host:workbench-host` inside the desktop app, and in a browser tab clicks a hidden anchor to `workbench://keep-awake` or `workbench://keep-awake?off=1` (one-way, the switch shows your last request)) and Widgets. Esc, an outside click or a screen change closes it.
- Create and plan: `+ Project` on Projects, `+ Card` on the Board head opens the board overlay card on Claude's draft form ("New cards"; `Write it myself` / `Draft with Claude` switch between it and the hand-written form, carrying the text; without `sample` it opens the hand-written form) (same height band and lock, Esc or the x closes and returns focus to the button). Keys: `n` on Board, `n` on Projects (ignored while typing).
  Plan's Claude call gets two read-only page tools where `sample.limits()` reports `tools`: `slack_find_channels` (`slack_search_channels`) and `slack_search` (`slack_search_public_and_private`, newest first, bots included), so it plans from what Slack says instead of asking; results are capped at 8000 characters with mail addresses removed, and the status line names each lookup. Without tools (or on `tools_unavailable` / `invalid_request`) it asks once more without them. Every proposed card carries acceptance items; a question only the user can answer goes in `question`, and such a card still moves out of Backlog.
  The project form's "Claude account for its cards" select writes `account` (`""` = Default: the helper's `workbench` account, or this PC's Claude login until it exists); its options are the accounts `card_sessions` reports (`accounts: [{ name, email, org, signedIn }]`), and every `card_start` passes the project's account.
  Each Projects row has Edit: the same form ("Edit project", slug fixed) updates `name, repo, body, account`; its Delete asks once more in the action row, then deletes the project's cards and the project doc, with a 10 s Undo toast that writes the raw docs back.
  `+ Project` writes `projects/<slug>` and switches the board to it; its Folder field filters a fixed-height list from `machine/folders` (name and parent path, click fills the path and an empty Name; typed paths are kept as they are; no list when the doc is missing); `+ Card` writes `cards/<slug>-<NNN>` (next id = max + 1; `ready` with acceptance, else `backlog`) with an Undo toast; `Plan` calls `sample.json` (capability `sample`, `modelTier: "default"`, `cache: false`, Stop button) for proposals, shows a checklist, and writes the ticked ones sequentially (log `planned with Claude`, Undo deletes them). Nothing is written until Add. `not_granted`, `sampling_disabled`, `not_declared` and `capability_*` hide Plan for the rest of the visit; the scrim does not close these forms.
- Open in the apps: every widget row, a meeting in the calendar pane and the deck's "Open" (Outlook / Slack) are `<button data-open="<app url>" data-fallback="<https url>">`. The click goes through `followLink`, which clicks a hidden anchor to the app URL when `settings.linkTarget` is `app` and to the https URL otherwise (no toasts). Builders: `slackAppLink` (`slack://channel?team=<config/board slackTeam>&id=<channelId>&message=<ts>`; without `slackTeam` Slack items use their web permalink for both targets), `mailLink(m)` (the web link for both targets: Outlook has no app URL we can rely on) and the event's `joinUrl`/`webLink`. Projects rows use `data-project`.
- Read state: mail and Slack watches refetch every 60 s (calendar 5 min). The DM and mention searches run with `include_context: true` (`max_context_length: 300`), so each hit brings the messages around it; a seventh watch, `from:<@me> after:<yesterday>`, adds your own messages. A conversation leaves the desk when your last message in it (or, for a top-level mention in a channel, your later top-level message there) is newer than its newest hit (Slack ts, compared as numbers), or when that hit is older than 24 h.
- Live feeds: `startFeeds()` runs once when `mcp` resolves, asks each connector who is viewing (`get_me`, then `slack_read_user_profile` for the Slack id that the mentions search needs, then `getAccessibleAtlassianResources` for the first Jira site; a failed lookup shows in that widget's footer and its Allow retries it), and registers the `watchTool` reads (calendar today and one per work day every 5 min, since the tool returns at most 25 events per call; after midnight the calendar reads move to the new day; 25 newest mail, Slack DMs, Slack mentions, Slack channel list and your own Slack messages every 60 s).
  The page turns each result into the `feed/*` docs below and writes a doc only when its content hash changed (or its `fetchedAt` is 25 min old, so footers stay truthful).
  Widget footers show the error code's copy (reconnect, add the connector, needs approval with an Allow button, not allowed) and keep the last doc; "Waiting for permission…" before the first result.
  Mail keeps unread, non-bulk messages; `direct` means you are among at most 5 recipients. Slack channels carry mention counts only (the connector gives no unread counts), so the Channels view lists channels with mentions.

## Docs the page reads and writes

Reads and writes (written by the live feeds above; a missing doc is an empty source, widget footers say "Not set up yet" without `mcp`):
- `feed/calendar`: `{ fetchedAt, url?, items: [{ start, end, title, location, joinUrl, organizer, attendeeCount, description }], week: { start, items: [...] } | null }` (`start` is the first work day) (`week` is null until all five weekdays were read; another week is read on demand and kept in memory only)
- `machine/night`: `{ cap: number | null, at }`, written by the usage pane, read by the helper's night check
- `machine/cards`: `{ model: "" | haiku | sonnet | opus | fable, effort: "" | low | medium | high | xhigh | max, ultracode: boolean, at }`, written by the dev menu's Cards run on and Ultracode, read by the page (every `card_start`) and the night check
- `feed/mail`: `{ fetchedAt, items: [{ id, from, subject, preview, bodyPreview, received, flagged, direct, to, cc, threadCount, url }] }`, newest first
- `feed/slack`: `{ fetchedAt, msgs: [{ conv, channelId, kind: "mention" | "dm", channel, thread, ts, at, from, fromId, text, url, hit }], channels: [{ name, unread, mentions, channelId, url }] }`: the 20 newest hits' conversations, at most 12 messages each, oldest first. `conv` is the DM's channel id or `<channelId>:<thread ts>`; `ts` is Slack's message ts, `at` local time; `hit` marks a DM to you or a mention. A doc in the older `items` shape still reads, as hits without context.
- `handled/<kind>:<id>`: `{ at }`; a Slack id is `slack:<conversation>@<newest waiting message ts>`. Older `handled/slack:<message ts>` docs still hide their messages.
- `triage/<docId>` (`docId` = the item id with characters outside `A-Za-z0-9_.~:@+-` replaced by `_`): `{ hash, urgency: now | today | later, kind: decision | reply | fyi | schedule, next: reply | schedule | read_later | archive | decide, confidence, gist, at, model: "claude" }`, written and read by this page (`gist`: Slack items only)

`start` and `end` may be `HH:mm` or a local ISO timestamp (the last clock time in the string is used). `fetchedAt` is epoch milliseconds or a local/ISO timestamp.
`received` and `ts` may be any display string; a timestamp from an earlier day shows as "Mon 17:10".
Links are only rendered when they start with `http://` or `https://`.

Writes from this page:
- `cards/<key>`: answer, send back, accept (with the chosen options in `picks`), Priority (`priority`), Move to (`status`, `updated`, `log`); `+ Card`, `Plan` and Looks good's "left for later" create the whole doc (`project, id, title, status, priority, created, updated, branch, goal, acceptance, question, answer, result, log`) and Undo deletes it
- `handled/<id>`: `{ at }` on Done and Got it
- `projects/<slug>`: `+ Project` creates `{ name, slug, status: "active", repo, color: "", body, created }`
- `triage/<docId>`: the shape above, written by Triage (`set`)
- `feedback/<YYYY-MM-DD>`: `{ items: [{ id, predicted, action, at }] }`, newest last, capped at 500; actions are `answer`, `looks_good`, `send_back`, `skip`, `done`, `reply`, `move:<status>`. Triage reads the last 7 days as calibration
- `feed/jira`: `{ fetchedAt, views: { mine | <config/board jiraViews key>: { total, url, items: [{ key, summary, status, cat, who, url }] } } }`, one `searchJiraIssuesUsingJql` watch per view on the `Atlassian MCP` connector (manifest entry), every 5 min; read only, never a deck source
- `data/users/<uid>/settings`: `widgets`, `widgetsOn`, `contextOpen`, `deferred`, `boardProject`, `linkTarget`, `keepAwake`

Code seams: one `state` object, `render()` dispatches on `state.view` and then syncs the board overlay, pure helpers sit at the top, the desk feed normalisers and `desk()` sit beside the data layer, input is one capture-phase handler for the open overlay plus one `keydown` dispatcher.
